Two main questions
  1. What is the Hilbert function for the module of splines on the chambers of generic hyperplane arrangement in real three-dimensional space? Preliminary notes and approach are outlined in Notes on presenting the non-trivial homology module for splines on fans in three dimensions . One of these notes is to prove that the module of continuous splines on the chambers of a three-dimensional central hyperplane arrangement is free if and only if the hyperplane arrangement is '3-generated.' This gives a starting point if the goal is to inductively construct free spline modules.
  2. Can we define a 'restriction map' for splines on the chambers of a hyperplane arrangement which yields 'addition-deletion theorems' for splines? Preliminary notes and approach are outlined in A restriction map and applications to freeness, with emphasis on hyperplane arrangements .
